Residential Architects

[01] About

We design homes that feel measured, personal, and deeply connected to the way people live.

Handshake is a residential architecture studio based in Seattle, working with homeowners, developers, and builders on intimate renovations, new homes, adaptive remodels, and larger buildouts that require calm direction and precise architectural thinking.

We believe a space is more than shelter. It is a daily frame for presence, beauty, function, and craft. Our work balances restraint with warmth, pairing careful planning with materials that age gracefully.

[03] Services

How we can help

We bring thoughtful modern architecture to new builds, additions, remodels, and reimagined interiors, giving every phase enough structure to move with confidence.

01

Site Research

Study site conditions, zoning context, light, access, views, and early constraints.

02

Schematic Design

Translate goals into massing, layouts, concept direction, and architectural priorities.

03

Design Development

Refine structure, materials, details, systems, and the feeling of each room.

04

Permitting & Documentation

Prepare drawing sets and coordinate the documents needed for review and approvals.

05

Construction Oversight

Support the build with site observations, clarifications, and design continuity.

06

Interior Detailing

Shape millwork, fixtures, finishes, lighting, and tactile moments with restraint.

07

Post-Occupancy Support

Review how the home performs after move-in and document future refinements.

Questions

Common Questions

Smaller studies may move in focused 3 to 6 month sprints. Full residential projects depend on scope, consultant needs, permitting timelines, and construction sequencing.

We help identify early constraints, prepare permit drawings, and coordinate with local review requirements so the design can move through approvals with clarity.

We can join an existing builder relationship or help evaluate fit, responsibilities, construction communication, and documentation needs before work begins.

Services can include site research, concept design, drawing sets, coordination, interior detailing, permitting support, and construction-phase guidance.

Renovation work begins with existing conditions, priorities, and budget alignment so the design can respect what is already there while making the home more useful.
